Privacy Policy

SparkStories (formerly referenced as AI Story Generator)
Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SparkStories. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the SparkStories iOS app and related services operated by Praxis Protocol / Goth Systems.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account information — email, username, and authentication identifiers when you register or sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Device information — device model, OS version, and a device identifier used for guest limits, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Usage and product events — actions such as story generation, quiz completion, subscription events, and similar product telemetry (including app version and platform).
  • Story and quiz content — prompts, generated stories, quiz answers, and related metadata needed to provide the service and improve quality.
  • Subscription and purchase data — handled by Apple In-App Purchase; we receive entitlement status needed to unlock Premium features. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Advertising identifiers — if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission, advertising partners may use an advertising identifier. You can decline tracking.
  • Biometric / device unlock signals (on device) — Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode may be used locally for the parent gate that protects kid reward settings. We do not receive or store your biometric templates; authentication is performed by the operating system.
  • Kid reward chart data (on device) — child display names, stamps, and reward settings you create for the local reward chart. This data is stored on your device only and is not backed up to your SparkStories account. Deleting the app or switching devices can erase it.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide story generation, quizzes, accounts, guest mode, and Premium features
  • Authenticate you (including Sign in with Apple and Google)
  • Enforce free-tier / guest limits and subscription entitlements
  • Show ads to non-subscribers (via Google AdMob), including personalized ads only if you allow tracking
  • Analyze product usage to improve reliability and features
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations
  • Enable optional parent controls for the on-device kid reward chart

4. Accounts, Guest Mode, and Deletion

You may browse as a guest with limited free stories, or create an account. If you create an account, you can request deletion from the Profile screen in the app. Deleting your account removes or disassociates account data we control on our servers, subject to limited retention needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. On-device kid reward data is not stored in your cloud account and may remain only until you delete the app or clear device storage.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted providers to operate SparkStories, including:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchases, and related platform services
  • Google — Google Sign-In (OAuth) and Google AdMob for advertising
  • Our backend services — hosted API infrastructure used for authentication, story generation, and product events

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. Advertising may be limited or non-personalized if you deny tracking permission.

6. Children’s Privacy

SparkStories is designed for families creating children’s stories. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 for accounts. Parent/guardian accounts and on-device kid reward profiles are intended to be managed by adults. Ads may be requested with child-directed treatment settings where applicable. If you believe we have collected information from a child inappropriately, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain account and service data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your Choices

  • Purchase Premium or Lifetime Access to remove ads
  • Deny or reset App Tracking Transparency permission in iOS Settings
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Delete your account from the Profile screen (when signed in)
  • Clear on-device kid reward data by deleting profiles in the app or uninstalling the app
